> **Audit Baseline:** GeoHarvest `v0.2.0` at commit `a6284d3` (`2026-11-13`)  
> **Classification Scale:** `P0` Critical (security exposure, data corruption, crash/instability) | `P1` High (core functionality broken/unreliable) | `P2` Medium (incomplete functionality, incorrect results, performance) | `P3` Low (usability, maintainability, docs)
